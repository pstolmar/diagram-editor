Menu & Food
What dishes are on the summer menu? ▾
Our Summer 2026 menu celebrates bold seasonal flavours sourced within 40 miles of the restaurant. Highlights include:
  • Seared Hand-Dived Scallops with mango and chilli salsa
  • Grilled Lamb Chops with za'atar, herb-roasted courgette, and pomegranate
  • Aubergine & Miso (vegan) — roasted aubergine, white miso glaze, sesame
  • Summer Pavlova with local strawberries, clotted cream, and elderflower
The full menu rotates weekly — our chefs adjust based on what's best at the market each Monday.

Do you have gluten-free options? ▾
Yes — we have 12 gluten-free dishes on our current menu, including starters, mains, and desserts. Our kitchen uses dedicated preparation surfaces for allergen-sensitive orders.

Chef's gluten-free recommendations: Pan-seared Sea Bass with saffron broth, and Roasted Cauliflower Steak with harissa butter.

Please inform your server of any dietary requirements when booking — we can adapt dishes where possible.

What vegan and vegetarian dishes do you offer? ▾
We offer a dedicated vegan tasting menu (4 courses, £65) available with advance notice. Our à la carte menu always includes at least 4 vegetarian and 2 vegan dishes, clearly marked on the menu.

Tell me about the tasting menus. ▾
We offer two tasting menus:
  • Seasonal 5-Course (£85) — showcases the best of each season's produce
  • Chef's Table 8-Course (£130) — Marco's creative vision, changes monthly
Both are available with optional wine pairing (+£45/£70). 48-hour advance booking required.

Bookings & Events
How do I make a reservation for a group? ▾
For groups of 6 or more, we recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance. Our private dining room seats up to 24 guests and can be arranged for exclusive hire.

Contact us directly at events@saffron-restaurant.com for group menus, dietary pre-orders, and event packages. We can also arrange bespoke wine pairings and chef's table experiences.

Do you offer private dining hire? ▾
Yes — our private dining room is available for exclusive hire, Monday to Saturday evenings. Capacity 12–24. Packages from £60 per person including a 4-course set menu. Contact our events team for custom packages and AV requirements.

Dining Experience
Tell me about the chef and kitchen team. ▾
Head Chef Marco Ferretti brings 18 years of Michelin-starred experience from Rome and London. His philosophy: "Let the season speak." Before Saffron, Marco held a Michelin star at L'Osteria Nuova in London for six consecutive years.

The kitchen team of 12 sources produce daily from farms within 40 miles of the restaurant. Marco visits suppliers weekly.
