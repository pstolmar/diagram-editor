Key Risk Disclosures

  • warning Market Risk

    The fund is subject to market risk, which is the possibility that the market values of securities owned by the fund will decline and that the value of fund shares may therefore be less than what you paid for them. Market values can change daily due to economic and other events (e.g., natural disasters, health crises, terrorism, conflicts and social unrest) that affect markets, countries, companies, or governments.

  • warning Interest Rate Risk

    Interest rate risk is the possibility that the market value of fixed income instruments owned by the fund will change due to fluctuations in interest rates. When interest rates rise, the market value of fixed income instruments may fall, and vice versa. This risk is typically greater for longer-duration instruments. The fund may be adversely affected by changing interest rate environments.

  • warning Liquidity Risk

    Liquidity risk is the possibility that the fund may not be able to sell a security or close out a derivative position at a desirable time or price. In unusual market conditions, even normally liquid securities may be difficult to value and to sell at a fair price. The fund's ability to sell such securities may be impaired, which could adversely affect NAV and total return.

  • warning Concentration Risk

    To the extent the fund's portfolio is concentrated in a particular sector, industry, geographic area, or type of investment, the fund may be more susceptible to losses due to adverse occurrences affecting that sector, industry, or geographic area. Investors should consider the additional risks associated with non-diversification before investing.
